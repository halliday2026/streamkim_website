  - question: "What is EMTALA, and what are a hospital's obligations under it?"
    answer: >
      The Emergency Medical Treatment and Labor Act (EMTALA) is a federal statute that
      requires hospitals with emergency departments to provide a medical screening
      examination to any person who presents, regardless of ability to pay, and to
      stabilize any emergency medical condition before transfer or discharge. Violations
      can result in civil monetary penalties and exclusion from Medicare participation.
      Stream Kim advises healthcare providers on EMTALA compliance and defends them in
      enforcement proceedings and civil litigation.

      rights or imposes additional obligations on covered entities. Stream Kim advises
      healthcare clients on state and federal privacy compliance as part of its
      healthcare regulatory practice.

## Healthcare Law in California

Healthcare providers in California face a litigation and regulatory environment that has grown steadily more complex. Stream Kim's healthcare practice reflects the firm's experience representing hospitals, ambulance services, medical groups, and other healthcare organizations in the matters that most frequently generate legal exposure: professional liability claims, regulatory enforcement, and internal disputes over ownership, employment, and contracts.

The firm's healthcare clients include institutional providers for whom litigation is an operational reality requiring experienced, cost-conscious management. Stream Kim approaches healthcare matters with the recognition that outcomes in individual cases affect broader institutional interests — staffing, credentialing, insurance, and public trust — and structures its representation accordingly.

The Inland Empire's healthcare sector has grown considerably in recent decades, with Riverside and San Bernardino Counties supporting a range of regional medical centers, specialty practices, and emergency medical services providers. Stream Kim's Riverside base gives the firm proximity to those institutions and familiarity with the courts and regulatory agencies they engage.

## Regulatory and Compliance Matters

In addition to litigation, the firm advises healthcare clients on the compliance questions that arise under federal and California law. EMTALA compliance, HIPAA and CMIA privacy obligations, licensing and credentialing matters, and healthcare employment law represent recurring areas where proactive legal advice reduces future litigation exposure.
