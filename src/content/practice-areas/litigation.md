  - question: "How does Stream Kim approach cases that may go to trial?"
    answer: >
      Stream Kim prepares every case for trial from the outset. This means conducting
      thorough discovery, developing the factual record early, identifying and working
      with experts, and building the trial narrative that will ultimately persuade a
      jury or court. This approach serves clients whether or not the case ultimately
      reaches a courtroom, because the credibility that comes from genuine trial
      readiness affects every aspect of litigation — including settlement negotiations.
  - question: "What is alternative dispute resolution, and when is it appropriate?"
    answer: >
      Alternative dispute resolution (ADR) encompasses mediation, arbitration, and
      other processes for resolving disputes outside the courtroom. Mediation involves
      a neutral mediator who facilitates negotiation between the parties; arbitration
      involves a neutral arbitrator who renders a binding decision. Many commercial
      contracts require arbitration of disputes, and California courts often refer
      cases to mediation. Stream Kim's attorneys are experienced in both ADR and
      litigation and can advise clients on which process is most appropriate for
      their particular dispute.
  - question: "How long does civil litigation typically take in California?"
    answer: >
      Civil litigation timelines in California courts vary significantly based on
      the court, the complexity of the case, and the parties' conduct during
      litigation. A straightforward matter in a less congested court might proceed
      to trial in 12–18 months. Complex multi-party cases in busy urban courts can
      take several years. Federal court timelines differ from state court, and
      many matters are resolved through settlement, dispositive motions, or ADR
      before reaching trial. A realistic assessment of the timeline is an important
      part of the early case evaluation Stream Kim provides to every client.

## A Litigation Firm

Stream Kim Hicks Wrage & Alfaro, P.C. was founded by trial lawyers and has maintained that character through more than two decades of growth. The firm's partners and associates approach civil litigation with the seriousness and discipline that comes from regular courtroom engagement — the kind of experience that shapes how cases are evaluated, how discovery is conducted, and how settlement negotiations are pursued.

The firm's litigation practice is not a separate department or a secondary function — it is the core of what Stream Kim does. Every practice area in the firm's work is served by attorneys who are prepared to try cases if that is what the client's interests require. This orientation toward trial readiness distinguishes Stream Kim's representation from firms that treat litigation as a last resort.

Stream Kim's litigators appear regularly in the courts of the Inland Empire, including Riverside Superior Court and San Bernardino Superior Court, as well as in the federal courts serving the region. The firm also handles matters in Los Angeles and throughout Southern California for clients whose disputes extend beyond the Inland Empire.

## Institutional Clients in Civil Litigation

The firm's client base reflects its founding character. Stream Kim has long represented the institutions — public agencies, healthcare systems, businesses — that face the most complex and consequential civil litigation. These clients require outside counsel who can manage high-stakes matters with discretion, cost efficiency, and genuine trial capability. That is the practice Stream Kim has built over more than two decades in Riverside.
