Eugene Kim has tried complex business and commercial matters to judgment and pursued appeals in California courts. His practice includes business litigation, partnership and ownership disputes, commercial real estate matters, franchise litigation, and civil rights defense. He takes matters from initial strategy through trial and post-trial proceedings, with experience representing clients before juries and courts across the state.

Mr. Kim has handled a broad range of commercial disputes over the course of his career, including healthcare practice ownership and valuation disputes, commercial equipment and lease matters, real property partition actions, franchise agreement controversies, employment claims, and civil rights litigation. His client base includes businesses of all sizes, healthcare practices, property owners, and individuals facing significant civil exposure.

He is based in Riverside and maintains day-to-day familiarity with the courts of the Inland Empire, including Riverside Superior Court and the federal district courts serving the Central and Eastern Districts of California.
