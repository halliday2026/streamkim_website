Theodore K. Stream co-founded Stream Kim Hicks Wrage & Alfaro, P.C. and has spent his career representing clients in civil litigation matters spanning multiple areas of California law. His practice reflects the firm's institutional character — complex cases that require experienced trial counsel who understand the stakes for large organizations, public entities, and healthcare systems operating in an increasingly demanding regulatory and litigation environment.

Over the course of his career, Mr. Stream has represented clients in matters involving civil rights defense, business disputes, public agency liability, and healthcare law. He brings to each engagement the judgment that comes from decades of California civil practice, with a particular orientation toward trial readiness and early case evaluation that shapes his approach from intake through resolution.

Stream Kim's Riverside base gives Mr. Stream an established presence in the courts of the Inland Empire, including Riverside Superior Court, San Bernardino Superior Court, and the federal courts serving the region. The firm's decades-long relationship with public agencies, healthcare providers, and businesses throughout Southern California is reflected in the breadth of matters Mr. Stream has handled throughout his career.
