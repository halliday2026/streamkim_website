Elizabeth Duran's practice at Stream Kim focuses on complex business litigation, where she contributes to trial preparation, motion practice, and discovery management across a range of commercial disputes. She approaches civil litigation with attention to the procedural details that often determine outcomes in complex cases, including evidentiary issues, dispositive motion strategy, and the development of the factual record through discovery.

Ms. Duran has experience in alternative dispute resolution contexts, including mediation preparation and arbitration proceedings. This work complements her litigation practice and reflects the firm's recognition that the skills required to litigate a matter effectively are also the skills required to resolve it efficiently.

She is based in Riverside and serves the firm's clients throughout the Inland Empire and Southern California.
