Mario Alfaro represents clients in complex civil matters requiring depth across multiple areas of law. His practice encompasses commercial litigation, business disputes, trade secret protection and misappropriation defense, real estate and property litigation, employment law matters, and appellate work in both California state courts and federal courts, including the Ninth Circuit. He brings cases from initial strategy through trial and, when circumstances warrant, through the appellate process.

Mr. Alfaro has represented banks and financial institutions in foreclosure-related litigation and large developers in contract disputes arising from commercial and residential projects. He advises contractors, developers, real estate agents, and brokers on the legal questions that arise in California's active real estate market, including construction contract disputes, easement and boundary issues, title disputes, and broker liability matters. His trade secret practice involves both offensive claims and defensive representation for clients facing misappropriation allegations.

His Riverside practice places him at the center of a region experiencing sustained commercial and residential growth, giving him practical familiarity with the land use, construction, real estate, and business disputes that characterize the Inland Empire's expanding economy. He appears regularly in Riverside and San Bernardino Superior Courts and in the federal district courts and appellate courts serving Southern California.
