Summer DeVore concentrates her practice at Stream Kim on complex civil litigation, with experience in business disputes, real estate matters, and litigation involving public entities and local government. She manages discovery, briefing, and law and motion practice in cases across California superior courts and federal district courts, contributing to trial preparation across the firm's practice areas.

Her work on behalf of municipalities and special districts gives her practical familiarity with the procedural and substantive issues that arise when public agencies are defendants in civil litigation. She works with the firm's public entity clients to manage the risks and exposures that come with civil litigation in the Inland Empire and across California, from initial pleadings through dispositive motions.

Ms. DeVore is based in Riverside and contributes to litigation teams across the firm's business, real estate, and public entity practice groups.
