Xiaoli "Shirley" Li brings an international legal perspective to the firm's business litigation practice. She earned her LL.B. from Tianjin University and an LL.M. (Magna Cum Laude) from the China University of Political Science and Law before pursuing advanced American legal training at the UC Irvine School of Law, where she earned both a J.D. and an LL.M. in American Law. She passed the Chinese bar examination in 2019.

Ms. Li's practice at Stream Kim focuses on business litigation in California courts, where she works on commercial disputes, contract matters, and civil litigation across a range of industries. Her Mandarin language fluency is an asset in representing clients and counterparties for whom Chinese is the primary language of business and in matters with cross-border dimensions involving Chinese commercial interests.

She is based in Riverside and serves the firm's clients in the Inland Empire and throughout Southern California.
