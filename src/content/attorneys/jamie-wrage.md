Jamie E. Wrage has built her career representing employers in the full range of employment disputes that California law generates. She has appeared before the Civil Rights Department, the Fair Employment and Housing Commission, Cal/OSHA, the California Labor Commissioner, the Workers' Compensation Appeals Board, the California Unemployment Insurance Appeals Board, the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission, and the U.S. Department of Labor. This breadth of agency experience reflects the regulatory complexity that California employers face and the firm's capacity to represent them across the multiple forums where employment claims are litigated.

Her litigation practice includes wage-and-hour class actions, Private Attorneys General Act (PAGA) matters, Cal-WARN Act claims, and harassment and discrimination cases. She also advises employers on preventive compliance — employment handbook reviews, wage-and-hour audits, and management training — as part of a practice philosophy oriented toward avoiding litigation through sound employment practices. The two aspects of her practice inform each other: the compliance work reflects what she has learned from defending employers at trial and before agencies.

Ms. Wrage is a co-founder of the firm's name and has practiced employment law in Riverside for more than two decades, serving as a consistent resource for the Inland Empire's employers on the evolving landscape of California employment law and the state's demanding enforcement environment.
