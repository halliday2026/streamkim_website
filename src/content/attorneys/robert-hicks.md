Robert Hicks co-founded Stream Kim Hicks Wrage & Alfaro, P.C. and has built a reputation for extensive courtroom litigation in business law matters. A graduate of the University of San Diego School of Law, where he served as an editor of the San Diego Law Review and graduated Order of the Coif, he brings rigorous analytical discipline to complex commercial disputes and brings cases to trial when that is what the client's interests require.

Mr. Hicks's litigation practice encompasses the range of business law disputes that California companies and individuals encounter: contract claims, business torts, partnership and corporate governance matters, and disputes arising from commercial transactions. He has represented clients across industries in California courts, with a particular orientation toward sustained courtroom engagement in matters that resist early resolution.

His practice is based in Riverside, where he and his colleagues serve the business community of the Inland Empire and clients throughout Southern California who require skilled trial counsel for complex commercial matters.
